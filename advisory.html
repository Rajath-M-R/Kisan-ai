Cultivation Practices

Land Preparation

Deep plowing followed by 2-3 cross harrowings. Level the field properly for uniform water distribution.

Timeline: 15-20 days before sowing

Seed Selection & Sowing

Use certified seeds (100-125 kg/hectare). Recommended varieties: HD-2967, PBW-725, DBW-187.

Timeline: October 15 - November 30

Fertilizer Management

Apply 120:60:40 NPK kg/hectare. Use organic manure 5-10 tons/hectare before sowing.

Timeline: At sowing and top dressing

Irrigation Schedule

4-6 irrigations required. Critical stages: Crown root initiation, tillering, flowering, and grain filling.

Timeline: Throughout growing season

Pest & Disease Management

Common Pests

  • Aphids - Monitor during flowering stage
  • Termites - Apply chlorpyrifos if needed
  • Army worm - Check during vegetative growth

Common Diseases

  • Rust - Apply propiconazole spray
  • Powdery mildew - Use sulfur-based fungicides
  • Loose smut - Use seed treatment

Preventive Measures

  • Regular field monitoring
  • Crop rotation with legumes
  • Use of resistant varieties
  • Proper field sanitation
